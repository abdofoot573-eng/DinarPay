## 1. Product Vision & Identity
**DinarPay** is an independent, next-generation Algerian Fintech platform engineered to compete with and surpass legacy postal/banking apps (such as BaridiMob) in user experience, financial intelligence, transparency, and reliability.
- **Brand Independence:** Strictly independent visual identity and brand (`DinarPay` / `دينار باي`). Zero impersonation of Algérie Poste or BaridiMob trademarks.
- **Regulatory Sandbox Separation:** Operates with a clear **Sandbox Simulation Mode** banner for immediate functional testing of RIP/CCP transfers, utility bills, mobile top-ups, cardless ATM withdrawals, and QR merchant payments, with a clean adapter boundary ready for licensed Bank of Algeria PSP/SATIM integration.

## 2. Target Audience & Localization
- **Primary Audience:** Algerian citizens, freelancers, merchants, and students managing daily DZD transfers, utility bills, and Gold/CIB cards.
- **Bilingual Support:** Native **Arabic (RTL)** and **Français (LTR)** instant in-app switching with zero layout breakage.
- **Accessibility:** Designed for both tech-savvy youth and non-technical users with minimum 48dp touch targets, one-tap Privacy Mode (balance masking), and unambiguous status feedback.

## 3. Core Differentiators Over BaridiMob
1. **Smart Financial Analytics Dashboard:** Automatic categorization of spending (Transfers, Bills, Top-Ups, ATM, QR) with visual breakdown bars and monthly cashflow summaries.
2. **Advanced Multi-Attribute Transaction Search:** Instant filtering by category, recipient name, reference code, or amount.
3. **Verifiable Digital Receipts:** Cryptographically structured receipt cards with reference code, fee breakdown, timestamp, and instant share/copy capability.
4. **One-Tap Privacy Mode (`وضع الخصوصية`):** Masks balance and sensitive RIP/card digits in public places with a single tap.
5. **Beneficiary Vault:** Save, favorite, and validate 20-digit RIP accounts to eliminate manual typing errors.
6. **Granular Card & Security Controls:** Instant card freeze/unfreeze, online & contactless toggles, and customizable daily transfer/withdrawal limits.
7. **24/7 Structured Support Desk:** In-app support ticketing with priority routing and instant protocol guidance.
